Analytics Dashboard Guide

Overview Panel

Total Inspections — All recorded inspections across all inspectors.
Compliance Rate — Percentage of inspections marked as Approved.
Active Inspectors — Inspectors with at least one inspection in the filtered period.
Occurrence Reports — Non-compliance incidents raised by inspectors.
Avg Days: Doc Send — Average days between inspection date and documents being emailed to client.
Avg Days: Approval — Average days between inspection date and approval.

Inspectors Panel

Quarterly Targets — Set per-inspector targets for each commodity per quarter. Click "Set Targets" to configure. Shows actual vs target with color coding (green = met, red = behind).
Performance Trend — Monthly inspection count per inspector over time.
Compliance Per Inspector — Breakdown of approved vs pending per inspector.
Directions Issued — Inspectors who issued directions (good inspector work identifying client issues).

Compliance Panel

Compliance Per Commodity — Approval rate for each commodity type (RAW, PMP, EGGS, POULTRY).
Daily/Weekly Trends — Compliance rate changes over time per commodity.
Samples Taken — Count of lab samples collected per commodity.

Operations & Financial

Operations — Travel distance (KM), hours worked, and efficiency metrics per inspector.
Timelines — Document sending turnaround times and approval speeds.
Financial — Revenue tracking, inspection fees, and cost analysis per inspector.

Filters

Use the filters at the top to narrow data by date range, year, month, inspector, or commodity. Click Apply to refresh all charts and KPIs. Click Reset to clear all filters. Extract downloads data as CSV. PDF exports the current view as a PDF report.
